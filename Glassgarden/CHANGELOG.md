# 1.0.0

- New Sunveil Audio granular pitch effect with a green pedal faceplate matching
  the six-knob layout, serif lettering, switches, and sun artwork of the family.
- Pitch from -24 to +24 semitones, six-second stereo capture, overlapping grains,
  three window textures, and fifth/octave companion voices.
- Freeze, reverse, feedback, stereo scatter, host tempo sync, and six presets.
- Details panel with tone, drift, width, low cut, pre-delay, and output controls.
- Cached native Win32/GDI+ editor, fractional knob dragging, Shift fine control,
  bounded incremental redraws, resize support, and host automation gestures.
- Independent VST3 IDs and versioned project state. Mono, mono-to-stereo, stereo,
  and 32/64-bit host audio supported. No lookahead on the direct signal.
